**Subject:** Power, Pressure & Choice — a few content clarifications before we start building

Hi team,

Thanks for the finalised Blueprint. As we plan the build, I've been working through the
document and the review comments, and a few points were either left open or need a
definitive call from you before we lock the content and begin development. Could you
confirm the following?

**1. Videos — in scope, and who owns them?**
Thuy suggested a *"short and engaging video"* for the introduction, and another for the
migration‑pressure explainer / the problem tree in Phase 2. Are these videos planned for
this release? If so: who produces them, and do they **replace** the intro/explainer steps
or **sit alongside** them? Our current plan is to build those parts as interactive screens
with text.

**2. Beliefs vs. attitudes — one step or two?**
Ali's causal model lists them as separate stages (*belief* "I must be in control" →
*attitude* "I must stay in control" → behaviour). To keep it simple for EAL learners,
we're planning to **combine beliefs & attitudes into one step** ("the soil"). Is that
acceptable, or would you prefer a distinct belief step and a separate attitude step?

**3. Anu — the toned‑down wording.**
Anu asked to "tone down" both the belief statements and the scenario. As we adapt them for
the game we'll be using softer, primary‑prevention framing (no depicted violence;
control / withdrawal / tension rather than assault). Could Anu confirm that direction — and
would she be available to review the **exact in‑game wording** once it's drafted?

**4. How much of the scenario detail should appear on screen?**
The approved "alternative story" includes some specifics: the eldest son wanting
independence, the wife wanting to study / work, and **the school noticing the children and
reaching out to support the family**. As we adapt the scenario for the game, should any of
these appear explicitly — particularly the school / support‑service beat, which is a
positive element — or should we keep it general for simplicity?

**5. Statistics — omit entirely, or allowed later?**
Anu advised **not** opening with family‑violence statistics (they cause disengagement), so
we're planning to open by normalising migration stress instead. To confirm: should the
stats Ali suggested (e.g. the $26 billion figure) be left **out entirely**, or would they
be appropriate **later** — for example on a closing "support & resources" screen?

**6. Migration‑pressure / loss‑of‑status explainer.**
Thuy asked Ali to focus this explanation on the scenario. Do you want a **dedicated
explainer screen** for "what is migration pressure / loss of status," and should it be
generic or specific to Mr. Alpha? Our current thinking is to weave it into the two pressure
steps and the tree‑model reveal rather than have a standalone screen.

**7. Multiple story pathways.**
Anu noted the approved story "can be **one of the pathways**." If you'd like more than one
scenario, it'd help to know roughly **how many**, and **what differs** between them (e.g.
same tree model, different family situations?), so we can plan the branching into the build.

Happy to jump on a quick call if that's easier.
